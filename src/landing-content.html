The fall of Mord Hive

Reyklund was worth a war because of what lay in its rock. Diamantine, refined in the great hive-refineries, bought the Imperium its loyalty and its enemies their interest. For years the planet bled for it.

Then the combatants left. Regiments embarked, warhosts withdrew, the Charter Masters loaded what they could carry and were gone. The world was abandoned to its fate, and Mord Hive, the last hive still being fought over, was struck from the void by a Space Hulk.

Mord Hive did not die. It broke. Hab-blocks lean over the chasms at angles no architect intended. Refineries smoulder. Somewhere above the smog, a few warp-capable ships still lie at anchor, and passage costs more than any survivor owns.

So the warbands go down into the ruins with lasguns and cinder blocks, to pry loose enough refined diamantine to buy a way off-world. Most of them, at least.

The way down

  1. The Spire

    The Charter Masters ruled from here. Their halls stand empty, and the landing pads above them are the only way off Reyklund.

  2. The Hab-stacks

    Ten thousand floors of homes, shrines and markets. The lights failed tier by tier. Those who stayed learned which stairs still hold.

  3. The Wound

    Where the Hulk struck. Its hull is fused into the flank of the hive, and neither has stopped burning. Nobody agrees on what came out of it.

  4. The Refineries

    Diamantine was cooked here by the tonne. The crucibles are cold, but refined shards still lie where the crews dropped them.

  5. The Underhive

    Old tunnels, lamp-lit shanties and Mag-Lev lines that somehow still hum. Most warbands make their camp here. Most never leave it.

  6. The Sump

    Everything the hive ever threw away ends in the black water. So do the catacombs. So, in time, do the warbands.

Every level has something worth dying for.

Why they stay

Escape

Most warbands are here to fund a way out. Every Throne they scrape together is a step closer to a berth on one of the last ships.

Plunder

Some came to sack what the armies left behind: refined diamantine in the rubble, Archaeotech in sealed vaults, the hoards of noble houses that fled.

Slaughter

And some have no interest in leaving at all. In a hive with no law and no witnesses, there are things that come only for the killing.

What lies in the ruins

After every fight your heroes search the wreckage. Most of the time it's rubble and a few Thrones. Sometimes it's this:

  • A sump hole with a shard at the bottom, and tainted water
  • A shattered refinery, still guarded
  • A Mag-Lev line that somehow still hums
  • A cargo-lift scrawled with sigils nobody should read
  • The underhive catacombs, and what waits in them
