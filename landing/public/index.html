The Setting

Hive World Reyklund (Vagorn Sector, Ultima Segmentum) was an Imperial diamantine-mining world; Mord Hive was its capital. After the fighting, an immense Space Hulk burst into realspace above the hive, dooming it. The abandoned hive is now fought over by isolated warbands, xenos, heretics and scavengers for caches of refined diamantine — the price of a berth on the last warp-capable ships.

Designer's Note

Setting from the Mord Hive v0.5 rulebook by Nick. Every statline, cost and rule in the app is transcribed from that book; anything the book doesn't settle shows as a visible TODO, never a guess.

In the App

Warband build

Pick any of the twelve warbands, recruit Heroes and Henchmen within the book's limits, and watch your Thrones and warband rating update as you go.

Datasheets

Every model as a datasheet: profile, skills from its own lists, and wargear held to the two close-combat, two missile weapon limit.

Trading Post

Buy Common and warband-exclusive gear into the treasury, sell it back at half price, laid out under the price chart's own headings.

Battle tracker

Roll or pick a scenario, then count turns, log events and tally who went out of action. A refresh at the table loses nothing.

After the battle

Serious injuries, experience, advances and income in one guided sequence. Nothing changes until you confirm it.

Rules reference

Skill lists, the wargear chart, psyker powers, prayers and each warband's special rules, searchable and built from the same data as your roster.

Fight Alone

Solo
  1. 01An app-built opponent. An enemy warband generated from any of the lists at the strength you choose, with its own casualty tally and suggestions for each activation.
  2. 02Temperament and a hidden agenda. Aggressive, Cautious, Cunning or Frenzied, and a secret objective you only see when the battle ends.
  3. 03The Oracle. Yes-or-no questions with a likelihood, a 2D6 reaction roll and a D66 hive-event table, dropped straight into the battle log.
  4. 04A generated battlefield. A seeded, re-rollable ruin layout shaped to the scenario.
  5. 05A solo campaign. An enemy agenda that runs across linked games, revealed act by act.

Designer's Note

The solo opponent, the Oracle and the battlefield generator are the app's own additions. They are not part of the Mord Hive ruleset and are marked as such in the app.
